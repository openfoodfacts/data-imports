# Producer Outreach

We are looking for volunteers to help engage food producers and manufacturers so they share their product data directly with Open Food Facts.

## How to Help

The outreach process and resources are documented on the Open Food Facts wiki:

- **[Producers' data – Open Food Facts Wiki](https://wiki.openfoodfacts.org/Producers%27_data)**

Following the steps outlined on that page, volunteers can help by:
- Reaching out to food brands and manufacturers (email templates and guides are on the wiki)
- Guiding interested producers through the Producer Platform or bulk import options
- Coordinating with the Open Food Facts team (`producers@openfoodfacts.org` or `#producers-platform` on [Slack](https://slack.openfoodfacts.org)) when a producer is ready to share data
